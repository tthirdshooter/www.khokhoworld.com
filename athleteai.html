How Computer Vision Helps Kho Kho

In Kho Kho, a millisecond makes the difference between a 'Kho' and a foul. Our **Live Form Analyzer** uses your device's camera to identify 17 key points on your body (shoulders, hips, knees, ankles). By calculating the angles between these points, the AI can provide instant feedback on whether your knees are sufficiently bent for a low dodge or if your back is straight during a sprint start.
